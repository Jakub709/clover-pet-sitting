2. Scope of the Service

  • 2.1. House sitting and in-home pet sitting means caring for the stated animals within the stated property for the agreed period.
  • 2.2. The service includes, where appropriate: feeding according to the Owner's written instructions; ensuring ongoing access to fresh, clean water; dog walking where it can be done safely and as agreed (including duration and location); appropriate exercise suited to the animal; companionship, supervision, and a caring presence in the home.
  • 2.3. In-home pet sitting means the Sitter stays at the property for the duration of the booking, including overnight, and provides ongoing care, companionship, and supervision for the pets. The Sitter may leave the property for reasonable periods for personal matters, provided this is within the maximum time the pets may be left alone, as agreed in advance with the Owner. Bookings that require supervision at all times, with no allowance for the Sitter to leave the property, cannot be accommodated.

  • 6.5. If off-lead walking is requested, responsibility for recall and behaviour remains with the Owner.
